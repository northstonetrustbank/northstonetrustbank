## 1. Your consent
By opening an account, you consent to receive the following electronically (by
email and/or in your online portal), instead of on paper:
- Account statements (including weekly statements)
- Deposit and transaction receipts
- Account approval, restriction, and status notices
- Loan/credit application decisions and disclosures
- Legal notices, updates to terms, and privacy notices

## 2. Hardware and software you need
To access electronic communications you need: a device with internet access, a
current web browser, a valid email address, and the ability to view and save PDF
or HTML documents.

## 3. Keeping your email current
You must keep your email address up to date. If emails to you bounce or fail,
notify us and update your details. Tell us immediately at
**support@northstonetrustbank.com** if you stop receiving expected messages.
