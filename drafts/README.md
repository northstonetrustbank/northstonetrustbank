# Northstone — Draft Legal & Marketing Content

**Status: DRAFTS for review. Not yet on the website.**

These documents are starting templates prepared so Northstone Trust Bank can
review the wording, fill in the blanks, and hand them to a licensed attorney
before anything is published.

## ⚠️ Read before using

- **These are not legal advice and are not final.** A qualified attorney (and,
  where relevant, a compliance officer) licensed in Northstone's operating
  jurisdiction **must** review and adapt every legal document before it is
  published or relied upon.
- A business that takes deposits and offers credit is subject to significant
  regulation. Depending on the country/state, this may include banking or
  money-services licensing, deposit-insurance disclosures, truth-in-savings /
  truth-in-lending disclosures, privacy law (e.g. GLBA, GDPR), electronic-
  signature law, anti-money-laundering (KYC/AML) rules, and more.
- Anywhere you see **[SQUARE BRACKETS]**, a real value must be filled in
  (legal entity name, address, jurisdiction, regulator, phone number, dates,
  bonus amounts, etc.).
